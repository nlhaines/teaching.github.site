How I teach

I design lessons to be fun and adapted to my students' interests. I meet my students where they are and give them agency in how they engage with the material and demonstrate understanding whenever possible. To facilitate this, I use my technical skills to build bespoke tools such as competitive games and choose-your-own-adventure style readings. For classroom management, I rely on routines, clear rules, consistent, calmly delivered consequences, and taking a genuine interest in my students as people.
